Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 6, 2026 · Last Updated: November 6, 2026 · Operator: Trust Node Logic

Welcome to Trust Node Logic. This Privacy Policy applies to all websites, web applications, and digital services operated by Trust Node Logic ("we," "our," or "us"), including trustnodelogic.com, hps.trustnodelogic.com, and the HPS Attestation Engine hosted at trustnodelogic.web.app (collectively, the "Application" or "Service").

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal data you entrust to us. This Privacy Policy details what information we collect, how we use it, our strict data minimization practices, and your individual privacy rights.

// 01. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum necessary information required to operate, secure, and deliver the Application:

A. Account and Authentication Information

When you sign in to our cloud applications using Google Sign-In or other OAuth identity providers, we receive basic profile credentials:

  • Your unique OAuth user identifier.
  • Your primary email address.
  • Your display name and profile picture URL (if provided by your identity provider).

B. Sovereign Local Audio Processing Architecture

Zero Server Upload Guarantee: When you analyze digital audio workstation (DAW) sessions, multitrack audio files, or stems using the HPS Attestation Engine (at trustnodelogic.web.app or hps.trustnodelogic.com), all audio parsing, SHA-256 essence hashing, and cryptographic signing take place locally in your browser sandbox using client-side WebAssembly and JavaScript. Your proprietary audio recordings, multitrack stems, and DAW project binaries are never uploaded, stored, or transmitted to Trust Node Logic servers.

C. Automatically Collected Technical & Operational Data

  • Device & Connection Data: Browser user agent, operating system, IP address, language preference, referring URL, and access timestamps.
  • Session State & Cookies: Essential first-party tokens stored locally on your device solely to maintain active login sessions and verify security tokens. We do not use third-party advertising tracking cookies.

// 02. How We Use Your Information

We use collected account and technical information solely for specific, legitimate operational purposes:

  • Authenticating your identity and provisioning access to cloud features.
  • Maintaining active session security and preventing fraudulent or unauthorized access.
  • Responding to technical support, verification inquiries, and service requests.
  • Monitoring application reliability, server diagnostics, and operational performance.
  • Fulfilling legal obligations and enforcing our Terms of Service.

// 03. Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

If you access our cloud application using your Google account, our use of Google user data is strictly governed by Google's developer requirements:

Limited Use Disclosure: Trust Node Logic's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade Google user data to any third party.
  • We do not use Google user data to serve advertisements, including personalized, re-targeted, or interest-based advertising.
  • We do not use or transfer Google user data to develop, create, or train generalized artificial intelligence (AI) or machine learning (ML) models.
  • We do not allow humans to read your data unless you have given explicit affirmative consent for technical troubleshooting, it is strictly necessary for security purposes, or it is required by law.

// 04. Sharing and Disclosure of Information

We do not monetize your data. We disclose information only under the following limited circumstances:

  • Infrastructure & Hosting Partners: Trusted enterprise infrastructure providers (such as Google Cloud Platform and Firebase) that host our web applications and manage authentication under strict data privacy and confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal Mandates: When required by lawful court order, subpoena, or applicable state or federal law.
  • Protection of Rights & Integrity: When necessary to investigate terms violations, protect the security of our users, or prevent malicious cyber activity.
  • Business Transitions: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or asset reorganization, user data would remain subject to the commitments made in this Privacy Policy.

// 05. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain user account credentials only for as long as your account remains active or as required by applicable law.

Account & Data Deletion Mechanism: You may request the immediate, permanent deletion of your account and any associated profile records at any time. To exercise this right, email trustnodelogic@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request." Upon verification, we permanently purge your account records from our production databases within 30 days.

// 06. Data Security

We apply robust administrative, physical, and technical safeguards to protect your personal data, including modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS) encryption for all network transmissions, isolated authentication credentials, and zero-storage architecture for user audio files. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage method can guarantee absolute security.

Depending on your location (including rights under GDPR in the European Economic Area and CCPA/CPRA in California), you possess specific legal rights regarding your personal information:

  • The right to know what personal data we hold about you and receive a copy.
  • The right to rectify inaccurate or incomplete personal records.
  • The right to request the erasure of your personal data.
  • The right to restrict or object to the processing of your data.
  • The right to non-discrimination for exercising your statutory privacy rights.

To exercise any of these statutory rights, contact us directly at trustnodelogic@gmail.com.
