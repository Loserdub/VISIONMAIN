The Industry Gap

Binary labels collapse process into a single bit.

A single "Was AI used?" checkbox creates two major failure modes across the industry today:

Failure Mode A — Over-Flagging

A songwriter tracks acoustic drums and vocals live, then runs an AI mastering plugin. They receive the exact same binary "AI" flag as a 10-second prompt generator.

Penalizes standard studio tools
Failure Mode B — Erasure of Craft

A producer spends 40 hours re-pitching, re-arranging, stem-separating, and re-performing an AI vocal seed. They are forced to hide their process or get branded as automated junk.

Erases human artistry
HPS does not ask "did you use AI." It evaluates who made the creative decisions at each stage — classifying the process, not the tool.

Classification Matrix

Three Tiers. The Hybrid Gradient is the Point.

Tier H — Human

Origination and performance are both human. Minimum 4 of 5 axes human. AI, if present, is restricted to post utility or corrective plugins.

Tier X — Hybrid

Primary human creative authorship on origination or performance with AI contribution on complementary axes.

X1  Human-Led — AI used for texture, sound design, or mastering only
X2  Co-Creative — Human & AI co-compose/sing; human retains edit control
X3  AI-Led — AI origination/synth; human substantially re-arranges & transforms
X4  Curated — AI origination/performance; human selects and lightly edits

Tier A — AI

Origination and performance are both AI-generated. Human input limited to text prompting and selection without structural rework.

PHASE 3 · AUTOMATED PIPELINE

Raw PCM Essence Hashing (`raw_audio_essence`)

HPS hashes decoded PCM audio samples, ignoring container headers. If ID3 tags are altered or format converted, the essence hash remains valid. If audio samples are edited, the hash invalidates automatically.
