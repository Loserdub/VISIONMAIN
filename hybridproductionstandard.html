CHAPTER 03 // THE FIVE PRODUCTION AXES
Executive Takeaway: Production is scored across five independent stages (Origination, Performance, Curation, Sound Source, Post-Production) as Human (H), Hybrid (H+A), or Autonomous (A).
Core Standard

The Five Axes of Music Production

Every work is scored across five core production stages as H (Human), A (AI), or H+A (Hybrid / Co-Creative):

H
Human Direct human physical execution, composition, acoustic tracking, or instrumental performance
H+A
Hybrid Co-creative collaboration, stem transformation, or AI-assisted performance
A
Autonomous Synthetic Direct generative neural synthesis without human structural intervention
Axis
Production Stage
Key Question
01 Origination
Composition & Writing
Where did the core musical idea come from: melody, chord progression, lyric concept, or structural seed?
02 Performance
Tracking & Vocal Execution
Who tracked the final audio: live physical instrument, vocalist, or MIDI performance in the master?
03 Curation
Arrangement & Editing
Who selected, chopped, stem-separated, and sequenced material into the master structure?
04 Sound Source
Timbre & Synthesis
Is the raw audio source an acoustic instrument/voice, analog hardware, or an AI synth model?
05 Post-Production
Mixing & Mastering
Who executed dynamic processing, EQ, spatial balance, and final audio mastering?

CHAPTER 05 // CLASSIFICATION TIERS
Executive Takeaway: Three deterministic tiers (Tier H Human, Tier X Hybrid Continuum X1-X4, Tier A Synthetic) capture the operational reality of modern music production without ambiguity.
Classification Matrix

Three Tiers. The Hybrid Gradient is the Point.

The production spectrum moves from unassisted acoustic execution to autonomous synthetic generation. Tier X classifies the entire operational reality of modern studio workflows.

TIER H (VERIFIED HUMAN)
TIER X (HYBRID SPECTRUM: X1 · X2 · X3 · X4)
TIER A (AUTONOMOUS SYNTHETIC)
VERIFIED HUMAN MASTER

Tier H - Human

Origination and performance are both human. Minimum 4 of 5 axes human. AI, if present, is restricted to post utility or corrective plugins.

HYBRID CONTINUUM

Tier X - Hybrid

Primary human creative authorship on origination or performance with AI contribution on complementary axes.

X1
Human-Led: AI used for texture, sound design, or mastering only
X2
Co-Creative: Human & AI co-compose/sing; human retains edit control
X3
AI-Led: Generative origination/synth; human substantially re-arranges & transforms
X4
Curated: Generative origination/performance; human selects and lightly edits
AUTONOMOUS SYNTHETIC

Tier A - AI

Origination and performance are both AI-generated. Human input limited to text prompting and selection without structural rework.

PROVENANCE ARCHITECTURE

Raw PCM Essence Hashing (raw_audio_essence)

HPS computes SHA-256 hashes exclusively over decoded, uncompressed PCM audio sample buffers, bypassing container wrappers and ID3 tags. Modifying tags, converting codecs (WAV to FLAC), or updating ISRC codes leaves the attestation hash completely intact. Altering musical audio content invalidates the hash instantaneously.
