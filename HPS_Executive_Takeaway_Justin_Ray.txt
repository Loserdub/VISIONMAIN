====================================================================
1. WHY HPS MATTERS NOW
====================================================================
On August 2, 2026, Article 50 of the EU AI Act mandates machine-readable
disclosure of synthetic audio. Flat "AI vs. Human" checkboxes fail modern
creators and platforms. HPS-1.0 provides a 5-axis process classification
and cryptographic verification layer that integrates seamlessly into existing
distributor DDEX ERN 4.3 pipelines.

====================================================================
2. THE FIVE PRODUCTION AXES
====================================================================
Each track is scored as H (Human), A (AI), or H+A (Hybrid/Co-Creative):

1. Origination: Melody, chords, lyric concept, structural seed.
2. Performance: Vocal execution, physical instrument, MIDI tracking.
3. Curation & Arrangement: Chopping, stem selection, sequencing.
4. Sound Source: Physical instrument/vocal vs. AI synth model.
5. Post-Production: Mixing, EQ, mastering assist plugins.

====================================================================
3. THE THREE TIERS & HYBRID GRADIENT
====================================================================
- Tier H (Human Production): Human origination & performance. AI confined to post utility.
- Tier X1 (Human-Led Hybrid): Human composition/vocal; AI used for texture/mastering.
- Tier X2 (Co-Creative Hybrid): Human and AI collaborate on writing or vocals.
- Tier X3 (AI-Led Hybrid): AI stem/seed; human substantially reworks & re-performs.
- Tier X4 (Curated Hybrid): AI composition; human selects and lightly edits.
- Tier A (AI Production): AI origination & performance; prompt-only output.

====================================================================
4. DISTRIBUTOR INTEGRATION (DDEX ERN 4.3)
====================================================================
- Zero-Build Ingest: HPS maps 1-to-1 into DDEX ERN 4.3 ArtificialIntelligenceDetails.
- Raw PCM Essence Hash: Hashes raw audio essence (raw_audio_essence SHA-256),
  making verification tamper-evident across metadata header changes.
- Cryptographic Proof: Ed25519 signed manifest (hps_manifest.json).

====================================================================
5. DSP PROGRESSIVE BADGING MODEL (SPOTIFY / APPLE MUSIC)
====================================================================
- Tier A: [Synthetic Audio] badge (EU Article 50 compliant).
- Tier X1/X2: Quiet UI integration inside existing "Song Credits" popups.
- Tier H: Optional [Verified Human] trust mark.
