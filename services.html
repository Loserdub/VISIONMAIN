FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common Engagement Inquiries

01 What is Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)? →

GEO structures your digital presence, JSON-LD schemas, and brand entities so AI engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews cite and recommend your brand.

02 What audio engineering services are offered? →

Justin Ray provides full multi-track stem mixing, stereo mastering, vocal tuning, spectral cleanup, and hybrid AI model audio processing for local and remote projects.

03 How do we begin a project or consultation? →

You can reserve a direct 1-on-1 strategy call using the Google Calendar booking button above or submit your project details via the contact form.

04 What are typical turnaround times and deliverables? →

Audio stem mixes and stereo masters typically deliver in 3 to 5 business days with included revision passes. Technical SEO and GEO audits deliver within 5 to 7 business days per domain.

05 How should remote clients prepare and send audio stems? →

Stems should be exported as 24-bit WAV files (44.1kHz or 48kHz), consolidated from bar 1 with all master bus limiters disengaged. Files can be shared via our secure transfer portal.
