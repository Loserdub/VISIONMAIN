Welcome to VØID 1.5
VØID 1.5 is a high-performance, browser-based Web Audio workstation. Runs 100% offline with off-thread Web Worker DSP processing.

Slice audio stems, build 16-pad performance kits, and generate intelligent Harmonic Auto-Mashups seamlessly.
1 : Slicer Tab
Slice & Extract: Drop up to 10 audio files. Choose granularities: Phrases, Bars, Beats, Triplets, Fixed Lengths (1s–10s), Section Auto-Detect, or Transient Hits.

Off-Thread ML: Automatic BPM, Camelot Key, & Structural Section detection without UI lag.
2 : Pads Tab
16-Pad Sampler: Drag slices onto pads and play live using keys [A]–[P] or auto-mapped MIDI controllers.

Tools: Customize pitch, volume, & choke groups, trim sample boundaries, record live sets, and export 1-click Ableton Drum Racks.
3 : Mashup Tab
Harmonic Auto-Mashup Engine: Blend multiple tracks using Camelot key matching, pitch locking, and sub-bass high-pass filtering.

Controls: Fine-tune with per-source weight & volume sliders, arrangement templates (DJ Set, Energy Arc, A-B Alt), and section filters.
