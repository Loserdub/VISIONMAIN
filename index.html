From the manifesto Manifesto Core // Foundational Thesis
"While people argue over black or white, human versus machine, or all or nothing, the real creative work happens in the grey. Hybrid production is about finding the soul inside the algorithm."

Hybrid production isn't about letting AI finish the track for you. It's about treating generative models as a starting point - seeding raw textures and ideas, then shaping them by hand with live instruments, analog signal chains, and real studio engineering.
